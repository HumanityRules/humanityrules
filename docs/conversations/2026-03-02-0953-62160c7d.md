## Best fit: **Settings > Organization** tab

This is the natural home for this setting. Here's why:

- **The page exists and is a placeholder.** `Settings > Organization` currently just shows "Manage your organization's general settings and preferences." — no real content yet. A "default org-role for new identities" dropdown would be its first real setting, giving the page purpose.

- **It's an org-level configuration, not an operational action.** The Security section (People, Groups, Policies) is for managing individual identities, groups, and policies — operational, day-to-day access management. A *default* that applies to all future joiners is a configuration/policy decision that belongs in Settings.

- **Follows platform conventions.** GitHub, Slack, and similar platforms put "default permission for new members" under org settings, not under the access control / members list.

## Considered alternatives

- **Security > People** (as a banner or header control) — tempting since it's where org-roles show up per-person, but mixing a global default into a list view creates confusing UX. Also, the People tab is for org-admins already managing individuals; the default is more of a "set it once" config.

- **New Security tab ("Defaults" or "Settings")** — keeps auth config together, but adds a fourth tab to Security just for one dropdown. Overkill until there are more security-level defaults to configure.

## What it would look like

On the `Settings > Organization` page, something like:

- **Organization name** (edit field, future)
- **Default org-role for new members** — dropdown with `viewer` (default), `member`, `admin` — stored on the `Organization` model

The setting would be read during identity auto-creation (when someone first authenticates via WorkOS SSO) to assign the corresponding `IdentityAttribute(key="org-role", value=...)` automatically.
