That's the only change needed. Here's what this does and why it's the better alternative:

**Before:** `_materialize_app_secrets` skipped any secret with `value: ""`, so optional secrets like `OPENAI_API_KEY`, `BRAVE_API_KEY`, `SLACK_BOT_TOKEN`, etc. were never provisioned in Secrets Manager.

**After:** All template-defined secrets are included in `app_secrets`:

- `value: "literal"` — stored as-is (unchanged behavior)

This is cleaner than treating `OPENAI_API_KEY` as a special case because it means **all** template secrets are visible in Secrets Manager from day one. The user can fill in whichever ones they need. Empty strings are effectively ignored by most SDKs (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc. all check for non-empty keys).

**For your existing openclaw deployment:** since the blueprint was created under the old logic, `OPENAI_API_KEY` isn't in its `app_secrets`. You'll need to either:
