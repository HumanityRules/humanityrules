How it works

Live in your cloud in
three steps

No new data center, no laptops to lock down. Point Humanity Rules at your cloud and roll out governed agents to your whole team.

1

Connect your cloud

Point Humanity Rules at your AWS account. We deploy into your VPC via CloudFormation — your perimeter, your keys, your data.

2

Set the rules

Choose your model providers, curate the skill catalog, and define ABAC policies and approval workflows. This is where humanity sets the rules.

3

Roll out to everyone

Every employee gets their own governed agent — reachable from Slack, the web, or a personal URL. Always-on, always audited.
