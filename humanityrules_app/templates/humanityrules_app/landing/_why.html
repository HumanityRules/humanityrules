Why now

Own your intelligence.
Don't rent it from a lab.

The labs are shipping memory now. Every project your team runs through their agents deposits a little more of how your company works somewhere you don't govern, onto their servers or into files on someone's laptop. The managed versions save you the setup. The trust boundary stays where it was.

Humanity Rules is the other side of that trade. We mean the name both ways: humans ruling the AI, and the rules it obeys. Neither is a promise; the whole thing deploys into an AWS account that belongs to you.

You keep the veto

The agent asks first. Anything sensitive waits for a person to sign off, however long that takes.

Memory you can move

What the agent learns is written to ordinary files on an EFS volume you control, yours to read or copy out.

Standard parts

The harness is open source and the stack is plain CloudFormation. If we disappeared tomorrow, your agents would keep running.
