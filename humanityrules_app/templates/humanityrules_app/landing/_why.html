Why now

Own your intelligence.
Don't rent it from a lab.

Models come and go. The best model was different last year, and there will be another one soon. Wire your company into one lab and you are betting it stays on top, under terms you don't write.

Companies already swap models by use case, and the labs know it. That's why this year's products are a level up: platforms where your agents run on infrastructure managed by the labs. That layer doesn't swap.

The alternative now has a name: AI sovereignty. Humanity Rules builds that layer for you in an AWS account you own, with the open source Hermes agent and CloudFormation you can read and modify.
