The Solution

Real agents for your team

Humanity Rules deploys a personal AI assistant for every employee inside your own cloud. Each one is isolated, always-on, and reachable from Slack, the web, or a personal URL — with permissions, approvals, and audit built into the platform.

An agent for everyone
