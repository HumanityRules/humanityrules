The problem

The agents are here.
How is your team running them?

You're scared to install it

And the fear is real: to run it, you hand over your files, your browser, your passwords. Everyone feels the same hesitation before install. Most people stop right there.

It only works while you watch.

The agent operates from a client app on your laptop. When you step away everything stops. An assistant that needs babysitting isn't much of an assistant.

It's built for hackers.

Config files, patches, a terminal. Brilliant if that's you. A wall if it's the rest of your company.

Unsupervised agents on company laptops, with company data. That's the version you have today.
