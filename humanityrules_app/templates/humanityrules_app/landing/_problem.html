The Problem

Your team is already using AI agents

You just can't see them. Or stop them.

Most agents today run on employees' laptops, wired to unvetted plugins and MCP servers, or on a vendor's platform you don't control. Either way, they read files, call APIs, and act on your systems: a second control plane outside your IAM, your VPC, your approvals, and your audit. Data walks out the door, nothing is logged, and you're locked into one lab's model and memory.

Data & credentials leak

Unvetted plugins and MCP servers can quietly exfiltrate secrets and company data. One prompt injection is all it takes.

No visibility, no audit

You can't see what employees do with their agents, and neither can your auditors. There's no trail to point to.

Running on someone else's account

Whether it's a laptop or a vendor's managed cloud, agents that run outside your own account are a non-starter for regulated, audited industries. Your data shouldn't leave your perimeter.

Locked into one lab

One model, one harness, non-portable memory. As agents learn your company, that memory gets bound to their platform. Not yours.

Shadow AI is already in your org. The only question is whether it's governed, or running loose.
