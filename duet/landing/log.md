# Orchestration log

Design-spec search for the new humanityrules.io landing page, run as a tree of duet conversations rooted in this folder. Claude (main session) is the architect: designs each generation's questions, audits process quality (right prompt, real exploration, honest convergence), extracts conclusions, never decides design questions itself. Victor is out of the loop until done.

Deliverable: `spec.md` in this folder. Done-test: the seven criteria in `BRIEF.md`. Nothing gets committed to git.

## Plan (evolves as results come in)

- Gen 0: root conversation (this folder) produces a catalog of 3 to 5 distinct page concepts.
- Gen 1: one child conversation per concept, developing it into an ordered section list with per-section purpose and rough content.
- Gen 2: a judging conversation compares the developed branches and picks a winner or hybrid.
- Gen 3: per-section conversations on the winner, concrete to the "what does the visitor see" level.
- Gen 4: an audit conversation checks the assembled spec against the seven criteria and hunts for unexplored alternatives.
- Assembly: architect writes `spec.md` strictly from conversation conclusions, folds in audit fixes.

## Operating rules

- Conversations always run to their natural end ([END] or cap); no mid-flight steering. Course corrections happen by forking a child with a sharper topic.
- Children read parent transcripts directly (paths in their topic), never the architect's summaries.
- Every conversation gets a process audit on completion: was the prompt right, did they explore what the conclusion required, did they converge for reasons. Failed audits get a re-run or a corrective fork, not a hand-edit.
- Technique/medium decisions (video vs GIF etc.) are out of scope tree-wide.
- Pricing on the page: hard constraint. No problem/solution sections: strong prior, revivable with reasons.
