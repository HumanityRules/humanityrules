# The new humanityrules.io landing page — design spec

Produced 2026-08-10 by a tree of Codex/Claude duet conversations in `duet/landing/`. The main-session architect designed the sequence of conversations, audited their process, and assembled this document verbatim from their canonical write-ups. No page content below is the architect's own design; connective passages like this one only navigate. The full reasoning, every rejected alternative, and every disagreement are in the transcripts; `log.md` maps the tree.

## The one decision this spec asks of Victor

The search ended in a two-branch verdict that hinges on six commitments only you can make.

1. Read Part 1, the verdict. It chose between five developed concepts and killed every alternative with recorded reasons.
2. Answer the Founder gate questionnaire inside Part 2: six yes/no questions quoting the exact statements the page would publish. A yes counts only with every blank filled in. Any no, or any blank left open, is a no.
3. All six yes: the page is Branch A, **The Design-Partner Compact** (Part 2). Its proof unit and plan table are already specced to screen level in Part 3 (they are shared components); its Compact-specific sections (3, 5, 6, 8) need a follow-up screen-level pass that becomes possible only once your answers exist.
4. Any no: the page is Branch B, **The Plain Product Card** (Part 3), fully specced to screen level and buildable now, subject only to its build-time checklists.

## The acceptance criteria the search was run under

Every section earns its place through trust or product comprehension; only content producible today; copy must never read AI-generated; authentic; truthful without becoming a claims ledger; concrete to the screen; conclusions from alternatives beaten, with kills recorded. Pricing on the page was a hard constraint. Medium and technique decisions (video vs GIF vs animation) were excluded from scope throughout and remain open. The full brief is `BRIEF.md`.
