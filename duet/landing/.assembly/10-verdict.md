## Lifecycle ruling

The Compact’s reinterpretation is accepted.

A literal maturity trigger would either promise uncommitted product work or rely on Victor’s subjective declaration of maturity. Q6 instead creates a checkable program boundary:

1. Applications close at the earlier of the instantiated cohort limit or date.
2. The Compact cannot renew.
3. The successor must choose one of three public dispositions.
4. The replacement page must publish the terminal external-partner count, including zero.

This does not prove maturity. It forces a public decision about maturity without inventing a roadmap. That satisfies the original gate’s purpose: finite duration, hosted-checkout coexistence, and a defined successor posture.

## Recorded disagreements resolved

### One Real Run versus Plain Product Card

The Card wins.

One Real Run retains the higher theoretical trust ceiling, but acceptance criterion 2 admits producible evidence, not hypothetical evidence. No qualifying run has been verified, and the concept’s own rules disallow approximation. The Card’s feasibility-adjusted ranking is therefore vindicated.

### Is the infrastructure owner the right pre-beta visitor?

Yes for the customer-cloud offer, but the Ownership Walkthrough is the wrong instrument today.

The infrastructure owner is the person who can authorize AWS access and accept HumR’s operating model. However, the Walkthrough makes today’s architectural liabilities its trust climax. It expertly qualifies the correct visitor and then supplies strong reasons to reject the product.

The Compact instead discloses the same decision-critical facts as the known conditions of a reciprocal pre-beta relationship. The audience is right; audit-first framing is premature.

## Hybrid ruling

The Compact is a deliberate, coherent hybrid:

1. It imports the Card’s compact product-proof unit.
2. It imports a bounded subset of the Walkthrough’s disclosure discipline.
3. Its trust climax remains the reciprocal compact—not product restraint or infrastructure tracing.

No further hybrid wins:

1. A Card with an unsigned application gate is scarcity costume.
2. A full Walkthrough inside the Compact would restore the qualified-rejection problem.
3. Meet the Builder’s continuity evidence does not exist.
4. No One Real Run beat is available to import.
